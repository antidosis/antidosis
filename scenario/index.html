READING THE REAL COAST

Coordination is the first lifeline.

This is an illustrative vision of Antidosis coordinating local exchange over a future LoRa mesh during an internet outage. It is not a live service demonstration or network-coverage prediction.

01Real place, simplified height

44 communities use geographic coordinates. Elevation, water, and named major roads are bundled from OpenStreetMap and AWS/Mapzen terrain sources. Terrain height is exaggerated tenfold for legibility.

02Routes have consequences

Relay failure removes a graph node and recomputes every new exchange. Added hops create scenario delays; missing paths isolate requests. Amber journeys follow eight bundled OSRM road routes.

03Prior agreements are different

The garden and hospital-ride examples are agreed online before the outage. Their physical journeys can continue. Six new requests need either restored internet or the future local mesh.

Today’s Antidosis marketplace requires internet access. Offline exchange and the LoRa mesh are part of the project’s design-stage vision. The exchanges are detailed illustrative scenarios, not current listings.
